Voice Bridge is now available. It gives your Google Voice Takeout archive a more useful way to browse: messages, calls, voicemails, and media together in one app.

Your archive stays local on your device. Voice Bridge is built for people who want to revisit or search through their exported history without handing that archive to another online service.

[Get Voice Bridge from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N6KNRV562N8?hl=en-us&gl=US&ocid=pdpshare).
