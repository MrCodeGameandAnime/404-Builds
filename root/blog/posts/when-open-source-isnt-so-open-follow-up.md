On October 20, I documented why I was ending my involvement with the enum work around MicroPython's `micropython-lib` PR #980. On October 29, fellow contributor Josverl responded publicly.

This is the next part of that record.

## A response from another contributor

Josverl thanked me for the work and expressed regret that the experience had led me to step away. He recognized the frustration and described my departure as a loss for the community.

He also clarified that my pull request targeted a personal fork, rather than the main MicroPython library repository, and encouraged me to distinguish that experience from the broader community. His suggested path for future work was a separate upstream pull request that brought contributions together with clear credit.

## The merge and the question of recognition

By the time of his response, my pull request into the fork had been merged. Josverl noted that development, while also questioning why I did not appear as the author of the resulting commit. That was his observation in the discussion, rather than a resolution of the authorship concern.

The response acknowledged the contribution, the frustration, and the importance of recognition. It also added a later development to the timeline: the pull request described as unmerged in my October 20 account had subsequently been merged into the fork.

The [original post](./when-open-source-isnt-so-open.html) records the experience that led to my withdrawal. This follow-up records the response that came afterward. Both source comments are linked so readers can follow the exchange directly.
