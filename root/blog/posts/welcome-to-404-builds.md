404 Builds is where failure meets creation. A digital foundry for the imperfect and the impossible. We turn ideas, edge cases, and “what ifs” into real products, experiences, and tools.

This blog is a place for the work behind those builds: project updates, experiments, and things learned along the way.

## We build what's missing

Software, games, and experiments all belong here. Sometimes an idea becomes a useful tool. Sometimes it becomes a game. Sometimes the experiment is the interesting part.

The projects are the result. This is a place to share the process.
